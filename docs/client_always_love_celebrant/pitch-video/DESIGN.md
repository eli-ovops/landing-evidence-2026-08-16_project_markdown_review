# Always Love · Private Proposal Film

## Concept angle

Botanical field notes meet a modern wedding editorial: real ceremony photographs are treated as evidence of attention, while concise typography names what was understood and what the redesign can clarify.

## Composition

- Focal element: one human, specific sentence per scene.
- Edge anchors: small Mudgee/Victoria metadata, hairline rules and scene indices.
- Supporting detail: one or two framed real photographs with full faces and bodies preserved.
- Background treatment: eucalyptus-charcoal field, restrained radial light, paper grain made in CSS, and oversized low-contrast ghost words.

## Motion

- Three calm scenes with distinct entrances and one ambient motion each.
- Focus-pull / blur-crossfade transitions at the two handoffs.
- Final scene may settle and fade at the very end; earlier scenes do not animate out independently.

## What not to do

- No synthetic people, face retouching or generic stock weddings.
- No beige/gold/blush template styling, glitter, script-font clichés or gradient text.
- No invented testimonials, prices, availability or service claims.
- No public agency pitch inside the customer-facing website.
