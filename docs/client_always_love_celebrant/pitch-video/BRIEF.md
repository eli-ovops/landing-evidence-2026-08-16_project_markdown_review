## Intent

A quiet, premium private proposal film—not a public advertisement. It should communicate three things without sounding generic: I understand Victoria's work across weddings, elopements, funerals and memorials; I respect the real stories and imagery already at the heart of her brand; and I want to help make the path from first impression to enquiry clearer.

## Assets

- Use selected real photographs and the existing public logo already staged in the local private demo.
- Preserve real people and their identities. Do not generate, replace or retouch faces.
- ImageGen was requested for one non-human botanical background, but the built-in service returned two server errors. Continue without synthetic imagery.

## Customizations

- 1920 × 1080, 18 seconds, no narration and no music. Deliberate silence keeps the piece personal and lets the words lead.
- Botanical editorial direction matching the private website concept: eucalyptus charcoal, cool pearl, dusty rose.
- Calm focus-pull / blur-crossfade handoffs; no flashy wedding-template effects.
- This film remains private and local. No publishing, upload or client contact.
