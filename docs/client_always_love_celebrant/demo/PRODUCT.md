# Always Love Celebrant Services · Private Demo Brief

## Intent

Create a premium, emotionally assured one-page concept for a Mudgee celebrant. The page should help couples feel Victoria's warmth, understand the ceremony options, and decide to enquire without relying on a form, booking system, CMS, or frequently updated content.

## Design read

- Register: brand-led editorial website
- Soul: 9/10
- Spectacle: 5/10
- Density: 4/10
- Hero engine: layered photographic depth with restrained pointer and scroll movement
- Direction: botanical cinema meets a modern wedding magazine

## Visual system

- Cool pearl backgrounds, eucalyptus-charcoal structure, one dusty-rose action accent
- High-contrast editorial display type paired with a crisp contemporary sans serif
- Generous but deliberate negative space, hairline borders, low-opacity grain
- At least four layout families: full-bleed hero, asymmetric editorial grid, portrait split, immersive gallery
- Avoid the predictable beige, gold and blush wedding-template treatment

## Conversion model

Primary action: `Enquire with Victoria` using the public `mailto:` address.

Secondary action: call the public phone number. No form, booking engine, availability calendar, payment, database, CMS, login, or server-side functionality.

## Content boundaries

- Stable service categories are presented without prices, availability, package counts, or year-specific booking claims.
- The local demo uses 20 of 25 non-duplicate core photo candidates from the current public site, plus two public logo variants.
- Public availability is not commercial reuse permission. Copyright and depicted-person consent remain pending client confirmation and block publication.
- Short public-site copy is adapted into original concise demo copy. No full-page or long-form source copy is reproduced.

## Acceptance for this stage

- Static production build succeeds.
- Desktop 1440px and mobile 390px browser checks show no horizontal overflow, broken assets, unreadable contrast, or console errors.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable non-essential motion.
- Every visible CTA is a genuine `mailto:`, `tel:`, or in-page link.
- Status remains L0 private demo, pending PMO and user acceptance.
