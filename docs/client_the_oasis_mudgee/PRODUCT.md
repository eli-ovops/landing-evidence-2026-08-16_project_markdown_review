# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mudgee and regional NSW clients comparing beauty and wellness services on mobile, often before deciding whether to call or continue into Timely. They need calm, trustworthy orientation rather than a complete live treatment catalogue.

## Product Purpose

Create a private, static one-page demo that shows how The Oasis Mudgee could explain its stable service families, founder-led care, trust and location before handing live service selection, pricing and availability to the existing Timely booking system. Success means a first-time visitor can understand the brand, feel reassured and choose a clear next action without any backend or ongoing content burden for us.

## Brand Personality

Quietly confident, restorative, attentive. The atmosphere should feel like entering a considered treatment room: polished and tactile, never clinical, showy or generic.

## Anti-references

- Generic beige spa templates with scripted serif logos, gold gradients and identical rounded cards.
- Cold medical-aesthetic clinics dominated by equipment, claims or clinical blue.
- Influencer-glam beauty pages with neon pink, excessive animation or crowded service menus.
- Pixel-for-pixel reproduction of the existing Squarespace site or reuse of its logo, photography, testimonials or long-form copy.

## Design Principles

1. Build trust before booking: positioning, service outcomes and founder-led care precede the Timely CTA.
2. Separate stable persuasion from live operations: prices, staff schedules and availability remain outside the static page.
3. Make calm feel deliberate: confident hierarchy, controlled pacing and tactile imagery instead of decorative clutter.
4. Respect the local decision: address, contact and the correct booking boundary remain easy to find on every device.
5. Original by construction: use generated or licensed imagery and original copy only.

## Brand Invariants For The Private Demo

- Preserve the supplied public The Oasis Mudgee logo asset without redrawing or restyling it.
- Preserve the declaration: “A sanctuary for self-love, specialising in all things beauty and wellness.”
- Preserve the three service families: “Advanced facials & massage”, “Lashes, brows & waxing”, and “Makeup, tanning & nails”.
- Keep the merchant-facing page in English; Chinese is used only for internal discussion.
- Do not reuse merchant photography, testimonials or additional long-form copy without separate confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and semantics, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, meaningful alt text, readable 390px layouts and a reduced-motion path for every animation.
