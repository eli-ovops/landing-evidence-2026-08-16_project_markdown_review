## Pattern map → original concept decision

| Reference pattern | Project decision | Originalisation / risk result |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate commercial proposition and industry specificity | Lead with “Commercial cleaning · Wagga Wagga” and a clear B2B promise | Original wording, no copied headline or layout |
| Sector pathways | Four compact sector cards for Offices, Medical, Retail/Hospitality, Education/Community | Abstract information architecture only |
| Trust through standards and operating approach | Three plain-language principles, no invented certifications, figures or reviews | Uses only concepts, not competitor claims |
| One visible enquiry route | `mailto:` and phone only | Deliberately omits form/CRM/data flow |
| Restrained editorial photography | Original ImageGen commercial-interior photo with no people, brands or competitor assets | Asset is AI-generated; final customer/public use remains human-confirmation pending |

## Rejected

No source code, logos, photos, testimonials, metrics, certification claims, case studies, client marks, brand names, typefaces or long-form copy were reused. Complex quote forms, account systems and portal-style functions are out of scope.
